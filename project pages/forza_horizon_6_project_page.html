Forza Horizon 6 | 01/09/2024 - 30/09/2026

I worked on Forza Horizon 6 and its first DLC as a Gameplay Engineer.

In the base game, the main features I owned were Auto Drive, Rush and Touge.

As of writing this the first DLC still hasn't come out, but I owned the new race mode. This feature was a culmination of my 3 years at PG, and despite it having complex gameplay and online mechanics I delivered it ahead of schedule and with minimal bugs.

Forza 6 received a 90/100 score on Metacritic , and you can check out its launch trailer here.

I had a good experience at PG, and everyone I worked with was great. I chose to leave not because of any animosity, but because I wanted to take my life on a different path. Now to see what the future brings...
